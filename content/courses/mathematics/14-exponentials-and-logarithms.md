# Exponentials and Logarithms

Exponentials and logarithms are inverse operations. The A-Level paper tests laws of logarithms, exponential growth/decay, and solving equations using logarithms.

### What you must know

- **Laws of logarithms:** $\log_a(xy) = \log_a x + \log_a y$, $\log_a(x/y) = \log_a x - \log_a y$, $\log_a(x^n) = n\log_a x$.
- **Change of base:** $\log_a x = \frac{\log_b x}{\log_b a}$.
- **Exponential growth/decay:** $y = Ae^{kt}$ where $A$ is the initial value, $k$ is the growth/decay constant.
- **Logarithmic graphs:** $\log_a y$ against $\log_b x$ straight line, or $\log_a y$ against $x$ straight line for $y = ka^x$.

### Worked example

**Solve $2^x = 8$.**

Step 1: Take $\log_2$ of both sides: $\log_2(2^x) = \log_2 8$.

Step 2: $x\log_2 2 = 3 \Rightarrow x = 3$ (since $\log_2 2 = 1$, $\log_2 8 = 3$).

### Worked example 2

**Solve $\log_2(x+1) = 3$.**

Step 1: Rewrite in exponential form: $x+1 = 2^3 = 8$.

Step 2: $x = 7$.

### Worked example 2

**Express $2\log_2 5 - \log_2 3$ as a single logarithm.**

Step 1: $2\log_2 5 = \log_2(5^2) = \log_2 25$.

Step 2: $\log_2 25 - \log_2 3 = \log_2\left(\frac{25}{3}\right)$.

### Common mistakes to avoid

- Confusing $\log_a(x+y)$ with $\log_a x + \log_a y$ (only products split).
- Forgetting the change of base formula when using calculators.
- Confusing $\log_a(x^n)$ with $n\log_a x$ (correct) vs $\log_a n^x$ (incorrect).

### Practice questions

1. Evaluate $\log_2 8$.
2. Express $\log_3 5 + \log_3 2$ as a single logarithm.
3. Solve $\log_2(x-1) = 3$.
4. Given $y = 5^x$, express $\ln y$ in terms of $x$ and $\ln 5$.

### Answers

1. $3$.
2. $\log_3 10$.
3. $x = 9$.
4. $\ln y = x\ln 5$.
