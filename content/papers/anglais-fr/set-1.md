## Structural Question Bank - Set 1

**Level:** Ordinary Level (Collège)
**Class:** Troisième
**Series:** Tronc Commun
**Subject:** Anglais
**Exam:** BEPC

---

## SECTION 1: GRAMMAR

**Q1.** Put the verbs in brackets into the correct tense: "When I (arrive) at the station, the train (already leave)." _(3 points)_

**Q2.** Turn into the passive voice: "The government built a new hospital in Yaoundé." _(3 points)_

**Q3.** Complete with the correct form: "If she (study) hard, she (pass) her exam." _(3 points)_

**Q4.** Choose the correct word: "He is (interested / interesting) in science." _(2 points)_

**Q5.** Turn into reported speech: "I am tired," she said. _(3 points)_

**Q6.** Complete with a relative pronoun: "The man \_\_\_\_ lives next door is a doctor." _(2 points)_

---

## SECTION 2: VOCABULARY

**Q7.** Give the opposite of: "cheap", "early", "success", "borrow". _(4 points)_

**Q8.** Give the synonym of: "happy", "big", "begin", "quickly". _(4 points)_

**Q9.** Complete the sentences with the correct word: "The doctor works in a \_**\_. The teacher works in a \_\_**." _(2 points)_

**Q10.** Match the words with their definitions: (1) library, (2) market, (3) hospital, (4) school. _(4 points)_

---

## SECTION 3: COMPREHENSION

**Read the text and answer the questions.**

_"Amina lives in Bamenda. Every morning, she wakes up at five o'clock, helps her mother prepare breakfast, and walks two kilometres to school. She loves mathematics and dreams of becoming an engineer. Last year, she won first prize in the regional science competition. Her teacher says she is the most hardworking student in the class. Amina believes that education is the key to a better future."_

**Q11.** Where does Amina live? _(2 points)_

**Q12.** What time does she wake up? _(2 points)_

**Q13.** How does she go to school? _(2 points)_

**Q14.** What does she want to become? _(2 points)_

**Q15.** What did she win last year? _(2 points)_

**Q16.** What does her teacher say about her? _(2 points)_

---

## SECTION 4: WRITING

**Q17.** Write a short paragraph (about 60 words) describing your best friend. _(5 points)_

**Q18.** Write a short paragraph (about 60 words) about what you did last weekend. _(5 points)_

## CORRIGÉ TYPE

**Q1.** "When I arrived at the station, the train had already left."

**Q2.** "A new hospital was built in Yaoundé by the government."

**Q3.** "If she studies hard, she will pass her exam." (First conditional)

**Q4.** "He is interested in science."

**Q5.** "She said (that) she was tired."

**Q6.** "The man who lives next door is a doctor."

**Q7.** cheap → expensive; early → late; success → failure; borrow → lend.

**Q8.** happy → glad/pleased; big → large/huge; begin → start; quickly → fast/rapidly.

**Q9.** "The doctor works in a hospital. The teacher works in a school."

**Q10.** (1) library = place with books; (2) market = place where people buy and sell; (3) hospital = place where sick people are treated; (4) school = place where children learn.

**Q11.** She lives in Bamenda.

**Q12.** She wakes up at five o'clock.

**Q13.** She walks two kilometres to school.

**Q14.** She wants to become an engineer.

**Q15.** She won first prize in the regional science competition.

**Q16.** Her teacher says she is the most hardworking student in the class.

**Q17.** Model: "My best friend is called Jean. He is tall and kind. He helps me with my homework and we play football together. He is very funny and always makes me laugh. I am happy to have him as my friend."

**Q18.** Model: "Last weekend, I visited my grandmother in the village. We cooked together and she told me stories about her childhood. On Sunday, I helped my father in the garden. It was a wonderful weekend."
