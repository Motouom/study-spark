## SECTION 1: GRAMMAR

**Q1.** Put the verbs in brackets into the correct tense: "She (live) in Douala since 2010." _(3 points)_

**Q2.** Turn into the passive voice: "They will open a new market next month." _(3 points)_

**Q3.** Complete with the correct form: "I wish I (can) speak English fluently." _(2 points)_

**Q4.** Choose the correct word: "There are (much / many) students in the classroom." _(2 points)_

**Q5.** Turn into reported speech: "Where do you live?" he asked me. _(3 points)_

**Q6.** Complete with a modal: "You \_\_\_\_ wear a uniform at school. It is compulsory." _(2 points)_

---

## SECTION 2: VOCABULARY

**Q7.** Give the opposite of: "always", "inside", "strong", "sell". _(4 points)_

**Q8.** Give the synonym of: "beautiful", "difficult", "small", "angry". _(4 points)_

**Q9.** Complete the sentences: "I need to buy some bread. I will go to the \_**\_. I am sick. I must see a \_\_**." _(2 points)_

**Q10.** Match the words with their definitions: (1) farmer, (2) nurse, (3) driver, (4) trader. _(4 points)_

---

## SECTION 3: COMPREHENSION

**Read the text and answer the questions.**

_"The city of Douala is the economic capital of Cameroon. It is a busy port city where many goods arrive from all over the world. Thousands of people work in the port, in factories, and in offices. The city is famous for its markets, especially the Sandaga market, where you can find everything from clothes to electronics. However, Douala also faces problems: traffic jams, pollution, and flooding during the rainy season. Despite these challenges, the people of Douala are known for their hard work and hospitality."_

**Q11.** What is Douala? _(2 points)_

**Q12.** Where do many goods arrive? _(2 points)_

**Q13.** Name one famous market in Douala. _(2 points)_

**Q14.** What can you find at Sandaga market? _(2 points)_

**Q15.** Mention two problems of Douala. _(2 points)_

**Q16.** What are the people of Douala known for? _(2 points)_

---

## SECTION 4: WRITING

**Q17.** Write a short paragraph (about 60 words) describing your town or village. _(5 points)_

**Q18.** Write a short paragraph (about 60 words) about your plans for the future. _(5 points)_

## CORRIGÉ TYPE

**Q1.** "She has lived in Douala since 2010." (Present perfect)

**Q2.** "A new market will be opened next month."

**Q3.** "I wish I could speak English fluently."

**Q4.** "There are many students in the classroom."

**Q5.** "He asked me where I lived."

**Q6.** "You must wear a uniform at school."

**Q7.** always → never; inside → outside; strong → weak; sell → buy.

**Q8.** beautiful → pretty/gorgeous; difficult → hard; small → little/tiny; angry → furious/annoyed.

**Q9.** "I will go to the bakery. I must see a doctor."

**Q10.** (1) farmer = person who grows crops; (2) nurse = person who cares for the sick; (3) driver = person who drives a vehicle; (4) trader = person who buys and sells goods.

**Q11.** Douala is the economic capital of Cameroon.

**Q12.** Many goods arrive at the port of Douala.

**Q13.** The Sandaga market.

**Q14.** You can find everything from clothes to electronics.

**Q15.** Traffic jams, pollution, and flooding during the rainy season.

**Q16.** They are known for their hard work and hospitality.

**Q17.** Model: "I live in a small village near Bafoussam. It is quiet and green. There is a market every Saturday where people sell vegetables and fruits. The people are friendly and helpful. I love my village because it is peaceful and beautiful."

**Q18.** Model: "In the future, I want to become a doctor. I will study hard at school and go to university. I want to help sick people in my community. I also dream of travelling to other countries to learn new things. I believe that with hard work, I can achieve my goals."
