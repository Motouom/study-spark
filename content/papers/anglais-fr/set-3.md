## SECTION 1: GRAMMAR

**Q1.** Put the verbs in brackets into the correct tense: "By the time we arrived, the film (start)." _(3 points)_

**Q4.** Choose the correct word: "This is the book \_\_\_\_ I told you about." _(2 points)_

**Q6.** Complete with the correct preposition: "She is good \_**\_ mathematics. She is afraid \_\_** dogs." _(2 points)_

## SECTION 3: COMPREHENSION

**Read the text and answer the questions.**

_"Cameroon is often called 'Africa in miniature' because of its great diversity. The country has more than 250 ethnic groups and two official languages: English and French. In the north, you will find the Sahel with its dry climate and the beautiful Mandara mountains. In the south, there are dense rainforests and the famous Dja reserve. The west is known for its green hills and the traditional kingdoms of the Bamileke and Bamoun people. Cameroonians are proud of their culture, their food, and their football team, the Indomitable Lions."_

**Q11.** Why is Cameroon called "Africa in miniature"? _(2 points)_

**Q12.** How many ethnic groups are there in Cameroon? _(2 points)_

**Q13.** What are the two official languages? _(2 points)_

**Q14.** What will you find in the north of Cameroon? _(2 points)_

**Q15.** What is the west of Cameroon known for? _(2 points)_

**Q16.** What is the name of the Cameroon football team? _(2 points)_

## CORRIGÉ TYPE

**Q1.** "By the time we arrived, the film had started." (Past perfect)

**Q2.** "The classroom is being cleaned by the students."

**Q3.** "It is important that every child go to school." (Subjunctive)

**Q4.** "This is the book that/which I told you about."

**Q5.** "He promised (that) he would help me the next day."

**Q6.** "She is good at mathematics. She is afraid of dogs."

**Q7.** polite → rude/impolite; possible → impossible; safe → dangerous; remember → forget.

**Q8.** important → significant/essential; clever → intelligent/smart; rich → wealthy; tired → exhausted.

**Q9.** "I want to eat some food/rice. I need some water."

**Q10.** (1) library = place with books; (2) laboratory = place for science experiments; (3) playground = place for playing; (4) canteen = place for eating meals.

**Q11.** Because of its great diversity (ethnic groups, languages, climates, landscapes).

**Q12.** More than 250 ethnic groups.

**Q13.** English and French.

**Q14.** The Sahel with its dry climate and the Mandara mountains.

**Q15.** Its green hills and the traditional kingdoms of the Bamileke and Bamoun people.

**Q16.** The Indomitable Lions.

**Q17.** Model: "English is very important in Cameroon because it is one of the two official languages. It helps people from different regions to communicate. English is also the language of business and technology. Knowing English opens many doors for education and work. That is why I study English seriously."

**Q18.** Model: "In my region, we celebrate the end of the harvest season. People wear traditional clothes and dance to the sound of drums. We share food and drinks with our neighbours. The elders tell stories about our ancestors. It is a joyful moment that brings the whole community together."
